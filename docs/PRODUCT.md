# Product & Commercial Truth — DMPES Sudamerican 5.4

Status: confirmed by project owner in conversation, except items explicitly marked pending. Version: planning baseline, 2026-10-10.

## Positioning
DMPES SUDAMERICAN 5.4 — Next Season is a PES 2017 mod with priority on South American football, including Brazilian Série A and Série B and other first/second divisions, while retaining selected major European and Asian leagues. Separate product/site from DMPES GLOBAL.

## Scope
- More than 500 clubs; 109 national teams across Europe, Africa, North/Central America, South America and Asia.
- Leagues: Argentina 1st/2nd, Peru 1st/2nd, Colombia 1st/2nd, Brazil Série A/B, Uruguay, Ecuador 1st, Chile 1st, other CONMEBOL clubs; Premier League, La Liga, Serie A Enilive, Bundesliga, other European clubs; AFC Champions League clubs, Saudi League, Rest of World and Classic teams.
- Mentioned tournaments: Copa América 2028, UEFA Euro 2028, UEFA Nations League 2026, AFC Asian Cup 2027, AFCON 2025, World Cup 2026, Libertadores 2026, Sudamericana 2026, UEFA Champions League 2026, Club World Cup 2025, Intercontinental Cup 2026, national cups/supercups. Verify final naming and availability before publishing; distinguish normal competitions from Completo-only extras.
