# Brand and Visual Direction

## Creative direction
**Hybrid cinematic + premium**. Make South American football the protagonist. Impactful real gameplay in hero and showcase; calm, clear, commercial UI for comparison, checkout and FAQ. Do not mimic an official game publisher's identity or imply affiliation.

## Colors
- Near black `#0A0E0A` (main background)
- Deep green `#0E3818`
- Brand green `#1E6B2E`
- Lime `#C8FF3E` (primary CTAs and selective accents)
- Off-white `#EEF2EA` (foreground)
Ensure WCAG-compliant contrast; don't use lime for all text.

## Type
- Anton for expressive headings
- Inter for body and UI
- Rajdhani optional for stats/labels
Load via optimized font mechanism; define fallbacks.

## Motion and media
- Cinematic hero using real provided gameplay footage or stills; poster image first, lazy-load video when possible.
- Respect reduced-motion settings. No sound autoplay. Avoid large videos on mobile networks.
- Authentic footage/screenshots only; decorative references cannot be presented as actual game captures.
- Use optimized image formats, responsive crops and clear alt text.

## Voice and copy
Brazilian Portuguese, confident, specific and natural. Avoid empty hype, fake urgency, invented ratings, guarantees and unsupported comparisons. Be transparent about subscription and requirements.

## Asset handoff
Owner reports logo, screenshots, gameplay footage, promo images and testimonials are available. Actual asset paths, permissions and approvals must be supplied in the repository. VSL is NOT ready and should have a replaceable slot, not block development.
