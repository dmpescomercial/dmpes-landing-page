# Landing Page — Information Architecture & Copy Brief

## Objective
Convert qualified visitors from social networks into paid LastLink checkouts while helping uncertain visitors contact WhatsApp commercial support. Secondary: clarify offer and reduce support friction. Mobile-first.

## Proposed route
Independent Sudamerican landing page. `/sudamerican` is a PROPOSAL, not an existing confirmed route. Final domain/route requires owner approval; DMPES GLOBAL must remain separate.

## Section order (initial proposal; test and adjust)
1. **Hero:** DMPES SUDAMERICAN 5.4 — Next Season. Positioning: "O futebol sul-americano é protagonista." Real gameplay poster/video, succinct proof points, primary CTA "Garantir meu acesso" (checkout), secondary CTA "Conhecer o DMPES" (scroll) or commercial WhatsApp.
2. **Product at a glance:** 500+ clubs, 109 national teams, 3 included versions. Distinguish stats from product endorsements.
3. **Leagues and tournaments:** browse by South America / Europe / Asia / National Teams; use verified competition names; emphasize Brazilian Série A/B.
4. **Real gameplay gallery:** stadiums, faces, kits, menus, match footage; only owner-approved media.
5. **Version comparison:** clear responsive table/cards for Completo, Lite, Mobile; reiterate all included in same R$49.99 package; extras and Master League Pro only Completo.
6. **Compatibility and storage:** PC requirements and mobile method only after verification; show version file/extraction space clearly; do not imply all phones run the Mobile version.
7. **Offer / pricing:** R$49.99 for 3 months; 3 versions, member area, updates, tutorials, Telegram support. Explicit renewal distinction between card/PIX Automático vs conventional PIX. Primary checkout CTA.
8. **How it works:** checkout via LastLink -> access member area -> download/install via tutorials -> Telegram support while active. Avoid unsupported instant-access guarantees.
9. **Social proof + FAQ:** authentic permissioned testimonials; FAQ about subscription, renewal, expiry, installed PATCH, versions, compatibility, downloads, support, cancellation/refund (only after policy validation).
10. **Final CTA / footer:** repeat price and subscription disclosure, checkout, commercial WhatsApp, legal links, independent-product notice.

## Interaction guidelines
- Lightweight competition tabs/filters; accessible keyboard navigation.
- Optimized gallery with responsive images, optional lightbox.
- Responsive comparison with no misleading omissions.
- Sticky mobile CTA only if it does not obstruct content or disclosures.
- Optional VSL module: configurable, initially hidden or placeholder; embed when approved and optimized. No fictitious video or claims.

## Conversion strategy
Primary action = LastLink checkout. Secondary action = WhatsApp commercial (pre-sale only). Telegram = post-purchase support information, not an acquisition CTA. Avoid repeated aggressive popups. Put recurring billing disclosure adjacent to each major price presentation.
